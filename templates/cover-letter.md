# Cover Letter Template

250 to 350 words, four short paragraphs, one page. Send it when it will be read and when you can
tailor it — [`../guides/06-cover-letters.md`](../guides/06-cover-letters.md) covers when that is.
A generic letter is barely better than none and spends the goodwill of the read.

Use the same contact header as the resume, so the pair reads as one package. Address a person where
you can find one; a team where you cannot; never "to whom it may concern".

---

```text
FIRSTNAME LASTNAME
name@example.com | +39 340 000 0000 | City, Country | linkedin.com/in/yourhandle

Dear [Name or Hiring Team],

[OPENING — two sentences. Name the exact role. Then the single most concrete reason you
are a fit. Not "I am writing to express my strong interest": that sentence is on every
recruiter's list of phrases that signal a generated letter.]

[PROOF — four to six sentences. One story, not a summary of your resume; they already
have your resume. Take the hardest requirement in the posting and show one instance of
you doing it, with a number and the constraint that made it hard.]

[FIT — two to four sentences. Why this company, in words that could not be pasted to a
competitor. Their product, a public engineering decision, their market, a specific team.
THIS PARAGRAPH IS THE ENTIRE EFFECT: if you can swap the company name and the letter
still makes sense, it is not a good letter.]

[CLOSE — two sentences. Direct, no grovelling. Name what you would want to talk about,
and state your availability.]

Regards,
Firstname Lastname
```

---

## The gap paragraph

Where you have something the resume raises and does not answer — a career change, an employment gap,
a relocation, a stated requirement you do not meet — this is where it belongs, and it replaces or
extends the FIT paragraph. Name it plainly in one sentence, then spend three on everything adjacent
that you have done. Stated once, without apology, it reads as confidence and it pre-empts the
objection instead of leaving it to be discovered.

## Before sending

- Swap the company name for a competitor's. If it still reads sensibly, rewrite the third paragraph.
- Search for the phrases in [`../references/action-verbs.md`](../references/action-verbs.md) under the
  generated-text group, and delete every one you find.
- Save as `Firstname-Lastname-Cover-Letter.pdf`, matching the resume's naming.
- Where the form offers a text box, paste plain text rather than attaching a file.
